# AWS

Deploy your application into your own AWS account. VectorIHub provisions and manages the compute and networking your container needs, using credentials you control.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your Own Account" icon="key">
    Resources are provisioned in your AWS account — you retain ownership and billing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Consistent Workflow" icon="check-check">
    The same Dockerfile-based deployment you'd use on any other supported provider.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

---

## Requirements

<Columns>
  <Column>
    <Check>An AWS account</Check>
    <Check>Credentials with sufficient permissions to provision resources</Check>
    <Check>A `Dockerfile` in your repository</Check>
  </Column>
  <Column>
    <Info>
      Connect your AWS account once in the dashboard — VectorIHub handles provisioning for every deployment you target at it afterward.
    </Info>
  </Column>
</Columns>

## Connecting AWS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Your AWS Account">
    In the VectorIHub dashboard, add your AWS credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Your Repository">
    Link the repository containing your `Dockerfile`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Your Deployment">
    Set your port, environment variables, and health check path.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy">
    VectorIHub builds your image and deploys it into your AWS account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Good fit for teams already standardized on AWS who want a consistent deployment workflow alongside their other infrastructure.</Tip>
