# VPS

Deploy your application to any virtual private server you own or manage — DigitalOcean Droplets, Linode, Hetzner, bare metal, or any other SSH-accessible host.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="You Own the Server" icon="key">
    Deploy onto infrastructure you already control, with no lock-in to VectorIHub-owned hosting.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Consistent Workflow" icon="check-check">
    The same Dockerfile-based deployment you'd use on any other supported provider.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

---

## Requirements

<Columns>
  <Column>
    <Check>SSH access to your server</Check>
    <Check>A supported Linux distribution</Check>
    <Check>A `Dockerfile` in your repository</Check>
  </Column>
  <Column>
    <Info>
      VectorIHub handles getting your container running and reachable on your server — you don't need Docker pre-installed.
    </Info>
  </Column>
</Columns>

## Connecting a VPS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Your Server">
    In the VectorIHub dashboard, add your VPS by providing its address and SSH credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Your Repository">
    Link the repository containing your `Dockerfile`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Your Deployment">
    Set your port, environment variables, and health check path.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy">
    VectorIHub builds your image and deploys it to your server.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Good fit if you already have a server you want to keep using, or you're cost-conscious about per-provider pricing.</Tip>
