*VectorIHub Development Journey · Part 4 of 8*

*June 22 – June 28, 2026 · Bring Your Own Cloud Architecture*

---

The biggest strategic shift happened this week: VectorIHub moved away from owning infrastructure altogether. Instead of forcing users onto VectorIHub-managed servers, users bring their own infrastructure — and VectorIHub manages the deployment onto it.

The new model is deliberately thin:

```
Developer → VectorIHub → Infrastructure Provider
```

This turns infrastructure providers into replaceable execution targets, and it defined VectorIHub's new identity as a *provider-agnostic deployment platform*. The product shifted from a Cloud Hosting Platform to a Deployment Infrastructure Layer.

It's a smaller surface area to own, and a far more valuable one: developers keep control of their own cloud resources, and VectorIHub focuses on making deployment consistent across whatever they choose.

**Up next:** how VectorIHub actually talks to all those different providers, consistently.
