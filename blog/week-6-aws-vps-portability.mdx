*VectorIHub Development Journey · Part 6 of 8*

*July 6 – July 12, 2026 · First Infrastructure Providers*

---

With a consistent deployment configuration in place, it was time to prove it could actually reach real infrastructure. This week VectorIHub began supporting real infrastructure — starting with AWS and VPS deployment, with more providers to follow.

The goal was to deploy the *same* application to *different* environments and get the same result: the same app, running the same way, whether the target was AWS or a VPS.

Seeing one unchanged application land cleanly on two unrelated providers proved the portability concept wasn't just an idea on a whiteboard — it was technically possible.

**Up next:** making it reliable enough to trust in production.
