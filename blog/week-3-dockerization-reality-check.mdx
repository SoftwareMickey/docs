*VectorIHub Development Journey · Part 3 of 8*

*June 15 – June 21, 2026 · Moving Responsibility to the Application*

---

This was the turning point. After two weeks of chasing automatic Dockerization, it became clear the effort was solving the wrong problem.

The ecosystem already had a mature container standard — Docker. So rather than trying to replace Docker workflows, VectorIHub could build on top of them.

The decision was simple:

> **Developers provide the Dockerfile, and VectorIHub handles deployment.**

That single change reduced complexity while increasing reliability, and it reframed the entire product — from *"VectorIHub builds your application"* to *"VectorIHub deploys your application anywhere."*

The architectural split that came out of this week still defines the platform today:

- **Docker** became the application packaging layer.
- **VectorIHub** became the deployment portability layer.

**Up next:** the biggest strategic shift of all — giving up on owning infrastructure.
