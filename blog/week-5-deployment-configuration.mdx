*VectorIHub Development Journey · Part 5 of 8*

*June 29 – July 5, 2026 · A Consistent Way to Describe Deployments*

---

Once VectorIHub stopped owning infrastructure, a new question took center stage: how does the platform communicate with different infrastructures without embedding the specifics of each one?

The answer was a provider-independent way to describe what an application needs — its image, ports, environment variables, resources, and health checks — without tying that description to any one provider's implementation.

In practice, that means you describe what your application needs once — its resource limits, its health check path, its environment variables — and VectorIHub handles getting your target infrastructure to actually provide it, whether that's a VPS or a cloud provider. The application definition itself never changes; only how VectorIHub fulfills it does. This week established the foundation for true multi-provider deployments.

**Up next:** turning that consistent deployment configuration into real infrastructure on two very different targets.
