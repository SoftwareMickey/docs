---
title: "beaver alie"
description: "Use Alie, Beaver's infrastructure assistant."
---

## Examples

```bash
beaver alie
beaver alie --resume [session]
beaver alie sessions
beaver alie session <session>
beaver alie approvals
beaver alie approve <approval-id>
```

Alie can explain findings and propose actions. Approval prompts and workspace policy control whether a proposed action can proceed.
