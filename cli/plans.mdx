## Examples

<Info>
  **Reviewed against source**: examples on this page were checked against `beaver-cli` source and its own `--help` output, not executed end-to-end against a live workspace.
</Info>

```bash
beaver plans
beaver billing plans --json
```

The table lists each plan's monthly price, its per-month price when billed yearly, and the price of an extra seat on plans that sell them. Below it, each plan lists what it includes (members, machines, retention). A plan that isn't on sale yet is not listed.

Use [`beaver billing`](/cli/billing) to price a team, inspect the subscription and seats, start checkout, cancel renewal, or view payments.
