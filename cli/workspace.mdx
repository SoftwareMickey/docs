## Purpose

A workspace is where your plan, billing, machines and projects live. Everyone starts with a personal workspace; a team
workspace adds members with roles. `beaver workspace` shows and switches workspaces, and manages the people, invitations,
approvals and ownership of a team workspace. Most solo users never need more than `beaver workspace show`.

## Syntax

```bash
beaver workspace <subcommand> [flags]
```

| Subcommand | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `show` | Show the active workspace: plan, your role, seats and limits. |
| `list` | List the workspaces you belong to, with your role and plan. |
| `use <name\|id>` | Make a workspace the active one for every later command. |
| `create <name>` | Create a team workspace (requires a plan that includes Team). |
| `members` | List the members of the active workspace. |
| `invite <email>` | Invite someone to the active workspace. |
| `invites` | List pending invitations, or revoke or resend one. |
| `accept <token>` | Accept a workspace invitation with the token from your invite email. |
| `role <member> <role>` | Change a member's role (owner, admin, developer, viewer). |
| `remove <member>` | Remove a member from the workspace. |
| `leave` | Leave the active workspace. |
| `activity` | Show what happened in the workspace, newest first. |
| `approvals` | List open approval requests; `approve` and `reject` decide them. |
| `transfer <member>` | Offer to hand the active workspace to another member (Owner only). |
| `transfers` | List ownership offers made to you, and this workspace's own pending offer. |
| `accept-transfer [id]` | Accept an offer to become a workspace's owner. |
| `decline-transfer [id]` | Decline an offer to become a workspace's owner. |
| `rename [<id\|name>] <new-name>` | Rename the active workspace, or another one you own. |
| `delete [<id\|name>]` | Delete the active workspace, or another one you own (Owner only). A team workspace must have no other members, no pending invitations and no projects first. |
| `recreate` | Recreate your personal workspace after it was deleted, with its projects. |

## Choosing the workspace a command acts in

Every command acts in exactly one workspace. Beaver picks it in this order, and stops at the first that is set:

| Order | Source |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | `--workspace <name or id>` on the command |
| 2 | The `BEAVER_WORKSPACE` environment variable |
| 3 | The workspace chosen with `beaver workspace use` |
| 4 | Your personal workspace |

`--workspace` works on every command, not just `beaver workspace`. A name that matches more than one workspace is refused and
the candidates are listed. `beaver workspace use personal` returns to your personal workspace. Membership is checked first
in every case, and the choice is remembered until you change it. In the interactive shell the prompt shows a workspace that
is not your personal one, for example `beaver [acme-team] ▸`.

If you are removed from the workspace you had selected, the next command says so and forgets the selection.

## Flags

### `show`, `list`, `members`, `leave`

No flags beyond the [shared flags](/cli/shared-flags). `--json` prints machine-readable output.

### `use`

Takes the workspace name or id as its argument. Use `personal` to return to your personal workspace.

### `create`

Takes the new workspace's name. It requires that you own a workspace on the Team plan.

### `invite`

<ParamField query="--role" type="string" default="developer">
  Role to grant: `admin`, `developer` or `viewer`. You cannot grant a role above your own, and an invitation cannot make someone an Owner.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--link" type="boolean" default="false">
  Create a shareable link instead of sending an email. The email argument is kept only as a label; the link is shown once,
  works once, lasts 24 hours, and needs the workspace's `allow_link_invites` policy turned on by an Owner.
</ParamField>

### `invites`

<ParamField query="--all" type="boolean" default="false">
  Include accepted, revoked and expired invitations.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--revoke" type="string" default="">
  Revoke the invitation with this id. Its seat is freed straight away.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--resend" type="string" default="">
  Resend the invitation with this id. It issues a new link; the previous one stops working.
</ParamField>

### `accept`

Takes the token from the invitation email. If you are not signed in yet, Beaver keeps the token, walks you through signing in, and
then completes the acceptance. You must sign in with the email address the invitation was sent to.

### `role` and `remove`

`<member>` is a name, an email address or an id.

### activity

<ParamField query="--since" type="string" default="">
  Only events after this time: an RFC 3339 timestamp, a duration such as `36h`, or days such as `7d`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--actor" type="string" default="">
  Only events by this member (name, email or id).
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--action" type="string" default="">
  Only this action, for example `member.invited`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--mine" type="boolean" default="false">
  Only your own activity.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--limit" type="integer" default="0">
  Maximum events to return.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--cursor" type="string" default="">
  Continue from a previous page.
</ParamField>

The feed merges audit events, deployments and operations. Owners and Admins see everyone's activity; other roles see their own.

### `approvals`

<ParamField query="--all" type="boolean" default="false">
  Include decided and expired requests.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--mine" type="boolean" default="false">
  Only requests you made.
</ParamField>

`beaver workspace approvals approve <id>` and `reject <id>` decide a request; both take `--note "…"`. Approving grants that one
action to the requester, who repeats their request. Nothing runs on your behalf.

### transfer

<ParamField query="--confirm-name" type="string" default="">
  The workspace's exact name. Asked for interactively when omitted; required with `--yes`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--leave" type="boolean" default="false">
  Leave the workspace when ownership changes hands, instead of staying as an Admin.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--yes" type="boolean" default="false">
  Skip the confirmation prompt, including the billing acknowledgement below.
</ParamField>

### `transfers`

<ParamField query="--cancel" type="boolean" default="false">
  Withdraw this workspace's pending transfer offer.
</ParamField>

## Consequences

- **`invite`** sends an email and reserves a seat until the invitation is accepted, revoked or expires (7 days).
- **`remove`** and **`leave`** take effect immediately: live connections close, API keys for the workspace stop working, and the
  next request is refused. Nothing the person created is deleted.
  See [Access, audit and removal](/concepts/team-access-and-audit).
- **`role`** takes effect within a few seconds.
- **`recreate`** exists as a recovery path, not routine maintenance. It does not restore plan or subscription history, only a
  fresh Free workspace.

### Handing a Team workspace to someone else

Only the workspace's primary **Owner** can offer it, and only to an **Admin or Owner** who is currently active. It is an offer, not
a hand-over: the person must accept within 72 hours, so nobody becomes the owner (and responsible for billing) by surprise.
Nothing moves when they accept: every project, container and setting stays where it is; only who owns the workspace changes.

**Billing.** A Team subscription is paid with the previous owner's payment method, so when the workspace has one, the command
tells you so and asks you to confirm. After the transfer the subscription (and any extra seat packs) stops renewing; the workspace
keeps its plan until the period already paid for ends, and the new Owner subscribes with their own payment method. A payment that
is failing blocks the transfer until it is resolved.

A person who owns a workspace that others belong to cannot delete their account until they transfer or remove the workspace.

## Examples

<Info>
  **Reviewed against source**: examples on this page were checked against `beaver-cli` source and its own `--help` output, not
  executed end-to-end against a live workspace.
</Info>

```bash Show the active workspace
beaver workspace show
```

```bash Switch workspace
beaver workspace list
beaver workspace use acme-team
```

```bash Invite, then check
beaver workspace invite ada@example.com --role developer
beaver workspace invites
```

```bash Change a role or remove someone
beaver workspace role ada@example.com admin
beaver workspace remove ada@example.com
```

```bash Recent activity by one person
beaver workspace activity --since 7d --actor ada@example.com
```

```bash Decide an approval request
beaver workspace approvals
beaver workspace approvals approve <id> --note "ship it"
```

```bash Hand the workspace to someone
beaver workspace transfer grace@example.com --confirm-name acme-team
beaver workspace transfers
beaver workspace transfers --cancel
beaver workspace accept-transfer
```

## Results & failures

| Situation | What you'll see | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The workspace does not exist, or you are not in it | `workspace not found` | `beaver workspace list`, then `beaver workspace use`. |
| Your access is paused | `membership suspended` | The Owner adds seats or resubscribes. |
| Your role lacks the permission | `insufficient permissions`, naming the permission and your role | Ask an Owner or Admin. |
| A full workspace invites someone | `Your plan allows up to 5 team members.` | Revoke an invitation, remove a member, or add seats. |
| An invitation cannot be used | `invalid or expired invitation` | Sign in with the invited address and use the newest link. |
| The invitation email could not be sent | `` The email could not be sent. Run `beaver workspace invites --resend <id>` to try again. `` | Resend it. |
| `transfer` with the wrong name | `NAME_MISMATCH` | Type the workspace's exact name. |
| `transfer` to someone who is not an active Admin or Owner | `RECIPIENT_NOT_ELIGIBLE` | Make them an Admin first. |
| `transfer` while an offer is waiting | `TRANSFER_PENDING` | Withdraw it with `transfers --cancel`. |
| Accepting after 72 hours | `TRANSFER_EXPIRED` | Ask for a new offer. |
| `transfer` while a payment is past due | `BILLING_UNRESOLVED` | Fix billing first. |
| Leaving as the last Owner | `you are the only owner: transfer ownership or delete the workspace instead of leaving` | Transfer ownership first. |

More messages, with fixes, are in [Troubleshooting Teams](/guides/troubleshooting-teams).
