## Examples

<Info>
  **Reviewed against source**: examples on this page were checked against `beaver-cli` source and its own `--help` output, not executed end-to-end against a live workspace.
</Info>

```bash
beaver workspace show
beaver workspace recreate
```

Most users only need `show`. Treat `recreate` as a recovery action and review its confirmation carefully.
