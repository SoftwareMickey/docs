## Purpose

`beaver billing` manages your workspace's paid plan — pricing a team, checking out to upgrade,
buying or stopping extra seats, cancelling a subscription, downgrading back to Free, and
reviewing payment history. Only the workspace **Owner** can change billing; an Admin can ask
the Owner with `request-upgrade` or `request-seats`.
[`beaver plans`](/cli/plans) is the standalone command for listing what plans are available;
`beaver billing` is everything that follows once you've picked one.

## Syntax

```bash
beaver plans [flags]
beaver billing <subcommand> [flags]
```

| Subcommand | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `plans` | List the plans you can use, with prices and what each includes (same as `beaver plans`). |
| `quote` | Price a plan for a team size before buying. |
| `subscription` | Show the current subscription, seats, billing owner and next charge, plus your plan and how much of each limit you're using. |
| `checkout` | Start a hosted checkout to upgrade (Developer, Team). |
| `seats buy` / `seats cancel` | Buy a pack of 5 extra seats, or stop renewing one. |
| `cancel` | Cancel the subscription's renewal. |
| `downgrade` | Move the workspace to Free, showing exactly what changes first. (`downgrade-free` and `downgrade-hobby` still work.) |
| `request-upgrade` / `request-seats` | Ask the workspace Owner to upgrade or add seats. |
| `payments` | List this workspace's payment history (Owner only). |

## Flags

### `checkout`

<ParamField query="--plan" type="string" default="developer">
  The plan to buy, for example `developer` or `team`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--cycle" type="string" default="monthly">
  Billing cycle: `monthly` or `yearly`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--gateway" type="string" default="" required>
  Payment gateway: `paystack` or `paypal`. Required.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--extra-seats" type="integer" default="0">
  Price this many extra seats (a multiple of 5) into the quote. Seats are bought after the plan
  is active, with `beaver billing seats buy`.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--yes" type="boolean" default="false">
  Skip the confirmation shown when the new plan replaces a subscription you already have.
</ParamField>

Price and plan are always resolved on the server — these flags only choose what you buy and
how you pay. Before anything is charged, `checkout` shows the priced lines.

### `quote`

<ParamField query="--plan" type="string" default="team">
  The plan to price.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--members" type="integer" default="">
  How many people the team needs. Defaults to the seats the plan includes.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--cycle" type="string" default="monthly">
  `monthly` or `yearly`.
</ParamField>

### `seats buy`

<ParamField query="--gateway" type="string" default="" required>
  Payment gateway: `paystack` or `paypal`. Required.
</ParamField>

### `request-upgrade` / `request-seats`

<ParamField query="--plan" type="string" default="" required>
  `request-upgrade` only: the plan to ask for.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--seats" type="integer" default="" required>
  `request-seats` only: how many extra seats, a multiple of 5.
</ParamField>
<ParamField query="--note" type="string" default="">
  A short note for the Owner (up to 300 characters).
</ParamField>

### `cancel` / `downgrade` / `seats cancel`

<ParamField query="--yes" type="boolean" default="false">
  Skip the confirmation prompt. See [shared flags](/cli/shared-flags#yes).
</ParamField>

## Consequences

- **`checkout`** opens a hosted payment page in your browser. The upgrade only takes effect
  once the payment gateway confirms it — `beaver billing subscription` may take a few seconds
  to reflect the change after you complete payment. If the new plan replaces a subscription you
  already have (for example Developer → Team), the old one stops renewing and runs until the end
  of the period you paid for; there is no refund or proration, and `checkout` says so and asks
  you to confirm first. Your workspace is converted in place: every machine, project and history
  entry stays where it is.
- **`seats buy`** adds a pack of 5 seats on the plan's cycle. **`seats cancel`** stops the pack's
  renewal; its seats stay until the period you paid for ends. Nothing is refunded.
- **`cancel`** stops future renewal charges (and any seat packs' renewals) but does **not**
  immediately revoke your plan's access — you keep it until the current billing period ends.
  This is not a refund.
- **`downgrade`** on a paid workspace is **scheduled**, not immediate: it shows which members
  would be paused (Free allows fewer), which retention limits apply, what is over the new
  limits, and which paid seats end — then, after you confirm, moves the workspace to Free at the
  end of the period you already paid for. Nothing is deleted. On a workspace with no paid
  subscription it enrolls the workspace on Free straight away.
- **`request-upgrade`** and **`request-seats`** change nothing and charge nothing; they send the
  Owner a request they can approve or decline.
- A member who isn't the Owner sees a plain explanation instead of a raw error, with the command
  to ask.

<Warning>
  Only the Owner can pay for, change or cancel a plan. An Admin who runs `subscription` still
  sees the plan, seats and usage, but not payment or renewal detail.
</Warning>

## Examples

<Info>
  **Reviewed against source**: examples on this page were checked against `beaver-cli` source and its own `--help` output, not executed end-to-end against a live workspace.
</Info>

<CodeGroup>

```bash List plans
beaver plans
```

```bash Check current subscription
beaver billing subscription
```

```bash Downgrade to Free
beaver billing downgrade --yes
```

```bash Upgrade
beaver billing checkout --cycle monthly --gateway paystack
```

```bash Price a team of 8
beaver billing quote --plan team --members 8
```

```bash Buy Team, yearly
beaver billing checkout --plan team --cycle yearly --gateway paypal
```

```bash Add a pack of seats
beaver billing seats buy --gateway paystack
```

```bash Ask the Owner (Admin)
beaver billing request-upgrade --plan team
beaver billing request-seats --seats 5
```

```bash Cancel renewal
beaver billing cancel --yes
```

```bash Payment history
beaver billing payments
```

</CodeGroup>

**Expected output** (`checkout`, reviewed against source):

```text
Opening: https://checkout.example/session/cs_example
```

**Expected output** (`subscription`, reviewed against source): your subscription status, then
your plan and how much of each limit you've used. A limit you're past is flagged.

```text
No active paid subscription — this workspace is on Free.

Plan:              Free
Machines:          2 / 2
Repositories:      1 / 5
Managed services:  3 / 2  — over limit (existing ones keep running; you can't add more)
Members:           1 / 1
Retention:         logs 3d · traces 3d
```

## Results & failures

| Situation | What you'll see | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `checkout` without `--gateway` | `--gateway is required (paystack or paypal)` | Pass `--gateway`. |
| `checkout` as a non-Owner | `… billing is managed by the workspace Owner. Ask them, or run: beaver billing request-upgrade --plan <plan>` | Ask the Owner, or send a request. |
| `checkout` when already on that plan or higher | `can't start checkout: the workspace already has this plan or a higher one` | Nothing to buy. |
| `quote` for a plan that isn't on sale | `no plan "<name>" is available to buy` | Run `beaver billing plans`. |
| `seats buy` without an active plan that sells seats | the server's reason | Buy the plan first; Developer doesn't sell seats. |
| `cancel` success | `✓ <message>` plus `Access until: <date>` | Renewal is off; access continues until that date. |
| `downgrade` on a paid workspace | the change list, then `✓ Downgrade scheduled — you keep your current plan until <date>` | Nothing changes until that date. |
| `downgrade` already scheduled | `A downgrade to Free is already scheduled for <date>` | Nothing more to do. |
| `downgrade` to anything but Free | `only a downgrade to Free is available` | Downgrade to Free, then buy the plan you want. |
| `downgrade` on a workspace with no subscription | `✓ Now on Free` | Workspace is on the free plan immediately. |
