Port, environment variables, and health checks are now configured once, independent of which infrastructure provider you deploy to. Your configuration stays the same whether you're targeting a VPS today or a cloud provider tomorrow.

In practice, that means you describe what your application needs once — its resource limits, its health check path, its environment variables — and VectorIHub handles getting your target infrastructure to actually provide it. The application definition itself never changes; only how VectorIHub fulfills it does. This established the foundation for true multi-provider deployments.
