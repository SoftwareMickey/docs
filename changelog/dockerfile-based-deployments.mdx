Replaced automatic Dockerization with a simpler, more reliable model: deployments now build directly from a `Dockerfile` you provide. This gives you full control over your build process and improves deployment reliability and scalability — Docker is now the packaging layer, and VectorIHub is the deployment layer built on top of it.

The decision was simple: developers provide the Dockerfile, and VectorIHub handles deployment. That single change reduced complexity while increasing reliability, and it reframed the product — from *"VectorIHub builds your application"* to *"VectorIHub deploys your application anywhere."*
