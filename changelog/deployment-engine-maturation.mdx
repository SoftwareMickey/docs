Deployments now move through clear lifecycle states — `pending`, `active`, `failed`, `terminated` — visible from your dashboard at every stage. We also improved reliability and observability across the deployment engine, so it's easier to see exactly what's happening during a deploy.

Proving the concept and running it reliably are two different jobs — this week shifted the focus to the second one. By modeling state at the platform level rather than the provider level, VectorIHub cleanly separated *application intent* from *infrastructure execution*.
