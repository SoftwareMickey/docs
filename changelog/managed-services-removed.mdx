Beaver now focuses on deploying and securing your applications on machines you own. It no longer runs managed databases, caches or brokers, so `beaver service`, the Services
section of the desktop app and the Managed services pages of the dashboard are gone.

## What to use instead

- **[Bring your own database](/data/bring-your-own-database)** — a hosted Postgres, MySQL or Redis, with the connection string held as a sealed variable.
- **[Run a database container](/data/run-a-database-container)** — the official image and a volume through Compose. Backups and upgrades are yours; the new
  [backup guide](/data/backups-yourself) shows a dump container and a restore.

## What does not change

Your containers and volumes keep running, and nothing of yours is deleted. Applications, Compose stacks, volumes, networks and domains behave as before.

Read the full notice, including answers to common questions, at [Managed services were removed](/reference/errors/services-removed).
