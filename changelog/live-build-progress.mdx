Watching a build happen used to mean seeing progress climb steadily and then stall for a stretch near the end, with no way to tell whether anything was still happening.

Build progress now reports every real step along the way — from pulling your code, through the image build itself, to pushing the finished result — and now carries all the way through to completion instead of going quiet right before the finish line.

This makes longer builds easier to trust: you can always tell the difference between "still working" and "something's wrong," even for a build that takes several minutes.
