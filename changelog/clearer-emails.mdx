The emails Beaver sends have been redesigned so that you can tell at a glance what happened, whether it needs you, and what to do about it.

## What is new

- **One layout for every email.** Verification codes, invitations, deployment results, billing notices and digests now share the same structure: what happened, the facts, then one clear action.
- **A reason in every footer.** Each email says why you received it and whether you can turn that kind off. Optional emails carry a **Mute these emails** link; required ones (security, ownership, payment failures) say that they cannot be turned off.
- **Names instead of IDs.** Emails name the deployment, machine, service or domain they are about.
- **A terminal command when there is one.** Where the next step has a `beaver` command, the email shows it.
- **Dark mode and plain text.** Emails follow your mail app's dark mode, and every email has a plain-text version.
- **Two new pages.** [Emails Beaver sends](/operate/emails-beaver-sends) lists every email, who receives it and which are required. [Is this email really from Beaver?](/operate/is-this-email-from-beaver) explains how to tell a real message from a fake.

## What stays the same

Your notification settings are unchanged, and so is what is required: security alerts, sign-in and 2-step verification messages, ownership changes and payment failures still always arrive. Beaver still never asks for your password or a verification code by email.
