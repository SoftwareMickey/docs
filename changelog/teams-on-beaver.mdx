Beaver now has a **Team** plan for people working together in a shared workspace.

## What Team gives you

- **Roles.** Owner, Admin, Developer and Viewer, with the exact permissions of each published in [Roles and permissions](/concepts/roles-and-permissions).
- **Safe production.** A new team workspace protects production by default: Developers ask before deploying there, and an Owner or Admin approves. See [Production safety and approvals](/concepts/production-safety-and-approvals).
- **An activity log** that records who did what, and every time a secret value is revealed. See [Access, audit and removal](/concepts/team-access-and-audit).
- **Shared Alie**, so a conversation can be shared with the workspace.
- **Immediate removal.** When someone is removed, their access ends at once and nothing they created is deleted.

## What it costs

**$59 a month, including five people.** More people are added in packs of five at $10 per person per month. Billed annually it is 20% less. [Plans and billing](/concepts/plans-and-billing) has worked examples.

## Upgrading is in place

Buying Team converts the workspace you already have. Every project, machine, service and setting stays where it is.
