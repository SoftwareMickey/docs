The plan you may know as **Hobby** is now called **Free** in the dashboard, the CLI and the API. It is the same plan, with the same limits, and nothing about your account changes.

## What changed

- Anywhere Beaver used to say "Hobby", it now says "Free": plan names, `beaver plans`, `beaver billing`, and the pricing pages.
- Internal identifiers are unchanged, so scripts and integrations that refer to the plan keep working.

## What stays the same

Your workspace, its projects, machines and services, and what you can run on the plan: two connected machines, five repositories, one deployable branch per repository.
