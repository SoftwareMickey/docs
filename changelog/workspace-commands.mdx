The Beaver CLI can now work with **team workspaces**. Update with `beaver update` to get them.

## What is new

- **`beaver workspace`** gains `list`, `use`, `create`, `members`, `invite`, `invites`, `accept`, `role`, `remove`, `leave`, `activity`, `approvals`, `transfer`, `transfers`, `accept-transfer` and `decline-transfer`, next to the existing `show` and `recreate`.
- **`--workspace <name or id>`** works on every command, and **`BEAVER_WORKSPACE`** sets it for a whole shell. `beaver workspace use` remembers a choice.
- **`beaver billing`** can price a team (`quote`), buy Team, add seats, and, for an Admin, ask the Owner with `request-upgrade` and `request-seats`.
- The interactive shell prompt shows the active workspace when it is not your personal one.

## What stays the same

If you never select a workspace, every command acts in your personal workspace exactly as before. Older CLIs keep working against the current service.
