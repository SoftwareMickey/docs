Each plan now sets how many **managed services** (databases, caches, queues and object storage) a workspace can have.

| Plan | Managed services |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 2 |
| Developer | 10 |
| Team | 25 |

## What this means for services you already have

**Nothing is deleted or stopped.** If you are already past your plan's allowance, every service you have keeps running exactly as it did. The only change is that you cannot **create another** one until you are back under the limit, by removing a service you no longer need or by moving to a plan with more room.

## Where you will see it

`beaver billing subscription` shows how many services you use against your limit, and marks a limit you are over as *over limit*. Trying to create a service past the limit tells you which limit you reached.
