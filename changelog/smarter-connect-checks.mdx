`beaver connect`'s Host-role check used to end with a blanket reminder to go verify your own port reachability. It now does that verification itself — confirming your ports are actually reachable from the internet, and only warning about the ones that aren't.

Environment detection is more accurate too: `connect` now reads a machine's actual form factor before falling back to guessing from whatever hardware happens to be present, so a desktop with a Wi-Fi card no longer gets misread as a laptop. Every capability check now shows the evidence behind its verdict, not just a pass or fail.
