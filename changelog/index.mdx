<Update label="v0.1.7 · Build Once. Deploy Anywhere." description="Jul 20 – Jul 26, 2026">

## Build Once. Deploy Anywhere.

VectorIHub's long-term direction is now official: **build once, deploy anywhere**. Our mission has shifted from simplifying hosting to making infrastructure interchangeable — the same application definition should work regardless of where it runs, today and as we add more providers.

This closes out the eight-week transition from a cloud hosting platform to a provider-agnostic deployment engine. Thank you to everyone who tested early builds along the way.

</Update>

<Update label="v0.1.6 · Deployment Engine Maturation" description="Jul 13 – Jul 19, 2026">

## Deployment Engine Maturation

Deployments now move through clear lifecycle states — `pending`, `active`, `failed`, `terminated` — visible from your dashboard at every stage. We also improved reliability and observability across the deployment engine, so it's easier to see exactly what's happening during a deploy.

</Update>

<Update label="v0.1.5 · AWS and VPS Support" description="Jul 6 – Jul 12, 2026">

## AWS and VPS Support

You can now deploy to your own AWS account or to any VPS you manage, using the same deployment workflow either way. Deployment execution is consistent across both — no provider-specific steps to learn.

</Update>

<Update label="v0.1.4 · Provider-Independent Config" description="Jun 29 – Jul 5, 2026">

## Provider-Independent Deployment Configuration

Port, environment variables, and health checks are now configured once, independent of which infrastructure provider you deploy to. Your configuration stays the same whether you're targeting a VPS today or a cloud provider tomorrow.

</Update>

<Update label="v0.1.3 · Bring Your Own Cloud" description="Jun 22 – Jun 28, 2026">

## Bring Your Own Cloud

Introduced Bring Your Own Cloud (BYOC): deploy onto infrastructure you already own, instead of a VectorIHub-managed environment. This marks our repositioning from a cloud hosting platform to a deployment layer that works across infrastructure providers.

</Update>

<Update label="v0.1.2 · Dockerfile-Based Deployments" description="Jun 15 – Jun 21, 2026">

## Dockerfile-Based Deployments

Replaced automatic Dockerization with a simpler, more reliable model: deployments now build directly from a `Dockerfile` you provide. This gives you full control over your build process and improves deployment reliability and scalability — Docker is now the packaging layer, and VectorIHub is the deployment layer built on top of it.

</Update>

<Update label="v0.1.1 · Dockerization Research" description="Jun 8 – Jun 14, 2026">

## Automated Dockerization Research

We spent this week investigating fully automatic Docker image generation across frameworks. The research surfaced a hard truth: automatic build systems introduce edge cases that are difficult to predict or support consistently at scale — a finding that directly shaped the direction we took next.

</Update>

<Update label="v0.1.0 · Foundation" description="Jun 1 – Jun 7, 2026">

## Foundation

Kicked off VectorIHub's deployment architecture — the initial workflow for turning a connected repository into a running deployment, and the early groundwork for an automated build pipeline.

</Update>
