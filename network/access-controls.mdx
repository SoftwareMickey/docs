"Access" is three different questions. Each has its own control.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">**Team** holds members, roles and approvals; a deployment's **Firewall** tab holds reach controls.</Tab>
  <Tab title="CLI">The `beaver firewall` and `beaver workspace` commands below.</Tab>

| Question | Control | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who on the internet can **reach** my app or service? | Visibility, firewall rules, bans, lockdown; your provider's port rules | [Firewall](/network/firewall), [Firewall requirements](/infrastructure/firewall-requirements) |
| Who on my team can **change** things? | Roles, protected production and approvals | [Roles and permissions](/teams/roles-and-permissions), [Production approvals](/teams/production-approvals) |
| Who can **sign in** as someone on my team? | 2-step verification (can be required per workspace), sign-in methods | [2-step verification](/teams/two-step-verification), [Sign-in and repository access](/teams/signing-in-and-repository-access) |

## Reach: make a service public or private

```bash
beaver firewall visibility dep_abc123                    # show
beaver firewall visibility set dep_abc123 --private      # internal-only
beaver firewall visibility set dep_abc123 --public
```

<Warning>
`--private` makes the service unreachable from outside your private network **immediately**. Confirm nothing external depends on it first.
</Warning>

Managed [services](/data) are never published on a public port; you reach them from your own machine through an authenticated tunnel
([Connect locally](/data/connect-locally)). To see what is publicly reachable on a machine and whether it is protected: `beaver machine exposure <machine-id>`.

## Reach: allow and block specific traffic

Firewall rules match IP, path, method, query or header and `allow`, `block`, `rate-limit` or `challenge`; the first matching rule in your priority order applies
([Rate limiting](/network/rate-limiting)). Ban a source with `beaver firewall ban`.

## Change: roles and approvals

A team workspace has four roles — Owner, Admin, Developer, Viewer. Developers deploy freely except into **protected** environments (production), where they ask an Owner or Admin. An
approval is a one-time permission for that exact action and you cannot approve your own request.

## Sign in: require a second factor

An Owner or Admin can require 2-step verification for the workspace; see [2-step verification](/teams/two-step-verification).
