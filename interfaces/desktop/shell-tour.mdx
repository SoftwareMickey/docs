## First run and sign-in

Choose **Sign in**; Desktop opens your browser to an approval page and completes the sign-in when you approve — you never type a password into the app. Details and the team-invitation shortcut are in [Install Beaver Desktop](/install/desktop). After sign-in you land on **Infrastructure** for your active workspace.

## The frame

<Shot name="desktop/infrastructure-home" alt="The Desktop window: the navigator on the left, the Infrastructure home in the middle, and the status footer along the bottom." />

Desktop is a window with a **navigator** on the left, the open section in the middle, an optional **Alie** rail on the right, a **terminal** that docks at the bottom, and a **status footer**.

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Navigator** | Switches section. It can contract to an icon rail without losing your place |
| **Section area** | A list (for example **Machines**), or the detail of the item you selected, with a breadcrumb back |
| **Alie rail** | The assistant — see [Approvals, Alie and traces](/interfaces/desktop/approvals-and-alie) |
| **Terminal** | The embedded terminal — see [Terminal and projects](/interfaces/desktop/terminal-and-projects) |
| **Footer** | Theme buttons (light, dark, system), **Terminal**, **Alie**, **Command ⌘K** and **Settings** shortcuts, the open project, and the **Control plane** indicator — hover it for *Beaver control plane connected* |

The top of the navigator holds the **workspace switcher**, showing the workspace, your plan and your role. **Alie** and **Settings** sit in a **System** group below the main sections, and your account — name, email and **Log out** — is at the bottom.

<Note>
The **Deployments** and **Observability** sections are **hidden from the navigator** in this build. The command palette still lists **Go to Deployments** and **Go to Observability**, but they are not yet part of the supported workflow and these guides do not cover them. For deployments, logs and metrics, use the Dashboard or the CLI.
</Note>

## The command palette

Press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux), or choose **Command** in the footer, to search commands, pages, machines and services — anywhere in the app. It lists **Recent** items first.

<Shot name="desktop/command-palette" alt="The command palette open over the Infrastructure home, listing Go to commands for each section and the Add Machine action." />

| Group | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Go to | Infrastructure, Machines, Beaver Cloud, Networks, Storage, Services |
| Do | **Add Machine**, **Create Cloud**, **Add Service** — each opens a sheet that shows the exact `beaver` command |
| Machine and cloud views | View logs, Open metrics, View connections, View capacity, View network, View services |
| Tools | **Open terminal here**, **Open Alie**, **Toggle Big Terminal**, **Open folder…**, **Close project** |
| Account | Sign in to a different account; **Use light theme**, **Use dark theme**, **Use system theme** |

Other shortcuts: **⌘⇧T** toggles the full-window **Big Terminal**; **Ctrl+`** shows or hides the terminal dock.

## Back and forward

Desktop keeps a history of where you have been, including the item you had selected, and offers **back** and **forward**. Switching workspace clears it, because a selection from one workspace means nothing in another.

## The workspace switcher

Choose another workspace from the switcher; the app shows **Switching to …** while it reloads everything for that workspace. A workspace that is **suspended** or gated shows a screen explaining why and what to do. Which workspace is active is remembered by Desktop only — see [How state is shared](/interfaces/how-state-is-shared).

## Plan card, suspended and gated screens

**Settings → Account** carries a **plan card**: the workspace's plan, seats and next charge as the server reports them, with one action for your role — an Owner opens web billing, an Admin sends a request to the Owner, and everyone else is told who to ask. The equivalent `beaver billing` command is shown under it.
If a workspace is **suspended** or a feature is gated by the plan, Desktop shows a full screen saying so and what resolves it, instead of a broken page. Desktop never calculates prices or charges.

## Updates

Desktop does not update itself. **Settings → Updates** shows the installed versions and the update policy, and [Install Beaver Desktop](/install/desktop) will carry the download when one is published.

## Banners and offers

Team notices appear as banners at the top of the window: an automatic workspace switch, a plan-state notice, a **pending invitation** to a workspace, and an **ownership offer** to become a workspace's Owner. Invitations and ownership offers each have **Accept** and **Decline** — an accepted ownership offer switches you into that workspace.
See [Invite members](/teams/invite-members) and [Leaving, removing and transferring](/teams/removing-members-and-leaving).
