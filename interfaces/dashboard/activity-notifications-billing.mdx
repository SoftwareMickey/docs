## Activity

**Activity** is the social side of Beaver: discover templates and builders, follow people, and publish your own. Its views are **Following**, **Explore**, **Saved** and **Workspace** (visible only to workspace members), plus **Preferences** and **Publish template**.

| Area | Shows | Actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Following** | Public activity from builders and templates you follow | **Follow**, reply, save |
| **Explore** | Trending templates, topics and suggested creators; sort the list | **Deploy**, **Fork**, **Share**, **View configuration** |
| **Saved** | Templates only you can see | Open or remove |
| **Publish template** | A flow that turns a project into a template | See [Publishing a template](/deploy/publishing-a-template) |
| **Your analytics** | Views, deploys, forks, saves, comments and signups via links, over a window you choose — only you can see them | — |

A **public profile** (reached from a builder's name) shows their templates and **badges** — such as the verified-creator badge — and you can **Block** or mute a creator. **Share** gives a link; opening a shared link lands on that template. Your own profile is under **Profile → Edit profile**. See [Activity and following](/operate/activity-and-following).

## Notifications

**Notifications** is your inbox. Tabs filter by **Unread** and **Read**; filters narrow by **Severity** (**Critical**, **Warning**, **Success**, **Info**), **Category** and **Workspace** (a single workspace, **Personal** or **All workspaces**). Open an item for details and its **Technical details**.
Some need an action — their state moves **Needs acknowledgement** → **Acknowledged** → **Resolved**, or **Expired**. **Mark all read** clears the unread count. Delivery preferences are in **Settings → Notifications**, with three tabs: **Email** (which emails you get, per category and, for a team, per workspace), **Schedule** (digest timing) and **Activity** (the social notifications that **Activity → Preferences** opens). Mandatory alerts are always on, and your choices are saved even if your email is not yet verified. See [Notifications](/operate/notifications).

## Billing

**Billing** has three sections — **Overview**, **Plan & seats** and **Payments**.

| Section | Shows | Actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | A billing summary: plan, status, next charge, billing owner, payment provider; usage such as seats used; recent payments | **Change plan**, **View all payments** |
| **Plan & seats** | Your current plan, included and extra seats, total capacity, members using seats | **Change plan**, **Add 5 seats**, **Stop renewing** extra seats, **Redeem a promotional code**, **Cancel plan** |
| **Payments** | Total paid, successful and pending payments, a table with date, amount, reference and provider | Search by reference; filter by status, provider and date; open a payment for details |

Choosing a plan opens **Choose a plan** and **Compare plans**, then **Review plan change** — which shows the new plan, the charge today and what changes — before a hosted checkout. A cancelled checkout shows **Checkout cancelled**: *no plan changes were made and you were not charged*.

<Note>
Only the **billing owner** can change the plan or buy seats. Everyone else sees a request button — **Request sent to the billing owner** — and the CLI has `beaver billing request-upgrade` and `beaver billing request-seats`.
</Note>

### What hitting a limit looks like

When a plan limit blocks an action, the screen or command says which limit and points to **Billing**; **Over capacity** appears on the seats section when members exceed seats. Resolve it by changing the plan, buying seats, or removing what you no longer need — see
[Plans and billing](/teams/plans-and-billing) and [Plan limits](/operate/troubleshoot-plan-limit).

<Warning>
**Resource Usage** and **Build Minutes** pages exist in the product but are not reachable in the Dashboard today. Plan usage appears on the home screen's **Usage** panel and in Billing.
</Warning>

## Same task in the other interfaces

Activity: `beaver follow`, `beaver activity`. Notifications: `beaver notifications`. Billing: `beaver billing`. Desktop shows notifications as desktop alerts and an approvals inbox; billing changes happen on the web. All tasks: [capability matrix](/interfaces/capability-matrix).
