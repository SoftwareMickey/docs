Every member of a team workspace has one **role**. The role decides what they can see and change in
that workspace, and it applies to the whole workspace.

| Role | In one line |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including money and ownership. Every workspace has at least one. |
| **Admin** | Runs the workspace: people, domains, firewall, machines, approvals, and everything a Developer can do. Cannot change the plan or hand over ownership. |
| **Developer** | Does the day-to-day work: create projects, deploy, operate containers, manage services, write secrets. Production is protected by default. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only. Sees the workspace, but never a secret value and never makes a change. |

## The full table

This table is generated from the same rules Beaver enforces, so it cannot drift from what actually happens.

## Rules that always hold

- **You cannot grant a role above your own.** An Admin can make someone a Developer or Viewer, but not an Owner.
  You also cannot change or remove someone who ranks above you.
- **There is always an Owner.** The last active Owner cannot be demoted, removed or leave. The workspace's
  primary Owner must hand ownership over first; see [Removing members and leaving](/guides/removing-members-and-leaving).
- **Money and ownership are the Owner's alone.** Only an Owner can buy, change or cancel a plan, or offer the
  workspace to someone else. An Admin who needs a plan or seats changed can send the Owner a request.
- **A Viewer never sees a secret value.** The names of environment variables are visible; the values are withheld.
- **You cannot approve your own request.** See [Production safety and approvals](/concepts/production-safety-and-approvals).

## What you will see when something is refused

| You are… | Beaver says | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A member, but your role lacks the permission | `insufficient permissions`, naming the permission and your role | Ask an Owner or Admin, or see who holds it in the table above. |
| Not a member of the workspace (or it does not exist) | `workspace not found` | Beaver answers identically for both, so nobody can probe which workspaces exist. |
| A member whose access is paused | `membership suspended` | See [Downgrading and what happens](/guides/downgrading-and-what-happens). |

In Beaver Desktop and the dashboard, controls you cannot use are disabled and say which role you would
need, instead of failing after you click.
