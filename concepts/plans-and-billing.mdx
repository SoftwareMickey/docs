Your Beaver plan sets usage limits. Billing covers what you're charged for **Beaver
itself** — separate from whatever your infrastructure provider (AWS, a VPS host, your own
hardware) charges you for the machines.

```bash
beaver plans
beaver billing
```

## The plans

Beaver has three plans: **Free**, **Developer** and **Team**. Free was previously called Hobby — same plan,
new name. Free and Developer are for one person; Team is for people working together in a shared
[workspace](/concepts/workspaces-and-teams).

All plans include the full deploy workflow — connecting a machine, deploying, inspecting a failure, and
redeploying. Deployments are unlimited on every plan. The plans differ in how much you can run, how long history is
kept, and whether you can work with other people, not in whether the basics work. Developer and Team include email
support.

Team adds what a group needs: [roles and permissions](/concepts/roles-and-permissions), a shared
[activity log](/concepts/team-access-and-audit), [production safety and approvals](/concepts/production-safety-and-approvals),
and a shared Alie.

<Note>
  Retention is how long Beaver keeps your logs, traces and activity log before they're cleared. It applies to history
  only — it never affects your running apps or your deployments.
</Note>

## What Team costs

Team is **$59 a month and includes five people**. More people are added in **packs of five**, at **$10 per
person per month** ($50 per pack). Billed annually it is **20% less**: $47.20 a month for the plan
($566.40 a year), and $8 per extra person per month.

| Team size | Monthly | Billed annually |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to 5 people | $59 / month | $566.40 / year ($47.20 / month) |
| 6 to 10 people, for example 8 | $109 / month | $1,046.40 / year ($87.20 / month) |
| 11 to 15 people | $159 / month | $1,526.40 / year ($127.20 / month) |

<Note>
  Extra seats are a second purchase. You buy the Team plan first, then add a pack of five with
  `beaver billing seats buy`. `beaver billing quote --plan team --members 8` prices a team of any size before you buy
  anything.
</Note>

Every member and every pending invitation uses a seat. Only the workspace **Owner** can buy, change or cancel a
plan; an Admin can send the Owner a request instead.

## Two bills, not one

Connecting your own machines means you pay your provider directly for them — Beaver's charge
is only for the platform. If your bill looks off, check which side of that split it's
actually on before assuming Beaver over- or under-charged you.

## What happens when a limit is reached

Reaching a limit blocks **adding** one more of that thing until you upgrade or free up room.
It never removes or stops something you already have — if your plan's limit drops below what
you're running, everything keeps running and you're only blocked from adding more.

`beaver billing subscription` shows your plan and how much of each limit you're using; a limit
you're past is marked *over limit*. Managed services follow the same rule: if you are over your plan's
allowance, existing services are never deleted or stopped; only creating a new one is blocked.

## When a Team plan ends

Cancelling keeps Team until the period you paid for ends. When it does, nothing is deleted: people beyond the new
plan's seats are **paused, not removed**, over-limit resources keep running, and subscribing again restores everyone.
See [Downgrading and what happens](/guides/downgrading-and-what-happens).
