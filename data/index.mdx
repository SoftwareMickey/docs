A **service** is a managed database, cache or broker that Beaver runs for you on your machines and attaches to your
application, so you do not install or maintain it yourself.

<Tip>
**Start here:** [Services and persistence](/data/services-and-persistence) explains the model; the
[catalog](/data/services) lists what you can create.
</Tip>
