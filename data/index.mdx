Beaver **deploys and secures** your applications on machines you own. It does **not** operate databases for you: there is no
managed database, cache or broker, and no backups, upgrades or failover run on your behalf. Your data lives where you choose.

You have two ways to give an application a database, and one thing to decide afterwards: who backs it up.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Bring your own database" icon="cloud" href="/data/bring-your-own-database">Use a hosted Postgres, MySQL or Redis from a provider you already trust.</Card>
  <Card title="Run a database container" icon="container" href="/data/run-a-database-container">Deploy the official image with a volume, next to your app. You operate it.</Card>
  <Card title="Connection strings and secrets" icon="key" href="/data/connection-strings-and-secrets">Keep the connection string out of your repository and image.</Card>
  <Card title="Back up your own data" icon="archive-restore" href="/data/backups-yourself">Dumps, volume snapshots and provider-native backups.</Card>
</CardGroup>
