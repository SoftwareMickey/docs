## Overview

**VectorIHub** is a deployment platform built around a simple idea: an application should be defined once and deployed consistently to any infrastructure.

Rather than another cloud hosting provider, VectorIHub sits between your application and your infrastructure. You describe how your app is built with a **Dockerfile**, and VectorIHub deploys it — consistently — to the infrastructure you choose.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a Dockerfile">
    Your Dockerfile describes your dependencies, build process, runtime, startup command, and exposed ports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Repository">
    Link your GitHub or GitLab account to VectorIHub.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Your Deployment">
    Set your port, environment variables, and health checks once in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Infrastructure & Deploy">
    Pick where your app runs — VectorIHub handles building your image and deploying it there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Dockerfile to use VectorIHub?">
    Yes. Your Dockerfile is how VectorIHub knows how to build and run your application — this keeps builds predictable and works for any language or framework that can produce a Docker image.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens during production updates?">
    We use blue-green deployments by default. Your previous version stays live until the new container passes its health check, so updates ship with zero downtime.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I move my app to a different infrastructure provider later?">
    Yes — that's the point. Your application definition doesn't change when your target infrastructure does.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
