**Beaver Desktop** is the desktop app for seeing your infrastructure at once — machines, Beaver Clouds, services, volumes and
networks — with a detail view for each. It works on the same workspace as the Dashboard and the CLI.

<Warning>
**Availability.** There is not yet a public installer or download page for Beaver Desktop, and the app does not update itself.
This page will carry the download and supported-platform list when they are published. Until then, Desktop is for people who
have been given a build. Everything else here describes what happens once you have it.
</Warning>

## Before you start

- A Beaver account (Google, GitHub or email).
- Optional: the [Beaver CLI](/install/cli) on the same computer. Desktop does not need it to run, but its Settings page shows
  whether the CLI is detected, and Desktop's action sheets show the `beaver` command for anything that would change something.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app and choose Sign in">
    Desktop opens your browser to a **Connect Beaver Desktop** approval page. You never type a password into the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve in the browser">
    Sign in there — with Google, GitHub or your email — and approve the connection. If your account requires 2-step verification,
    the app tells you it is waiting for it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to the app">
    Desktop completes the sign-in and opens the **Infrastructure** home for your active workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
If someone invited you to a team workspace, open the invitation link on the same computer, sign in with the email address the
invitation was sent to, and Desktop accepts it for you.
</Tip>

## First launch

With no machines enrolled, the Infrastructure home shows an empty state that points you to **enrol your first machine** —
which you do in a terminal with [`beaver connect`](/infrastructure/connect-a-machine). Once a machine is connected it appears
here as a card with its health and what it is running.

## Updates

Desktop has no automatic update mechanism today. **Settings → Updates** shows the installed version and whether the Beaver CLI
is on your `PATH`.
