## What you'll do

Beaver Desktop is mainly a place to **look at and understand** your infrastructure. This tutorial walks the app once so you know where
things are. To change things you will use the CLI or the Dashboard — Desktop shows you the matching command.

## Before you start

- Beaver Desktop [installed and signed in](/install/desktop) (availability is limited — see that page).
- At least one machine connected. If you have none, do the [CLI quickstart](/start/quickstart-cli) first; Desktop cannot enrol one.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the Infrastructure home">
    The first screen after sign-in. It counts your machines, clouds and services, and shows a card per machine with its health.
    An empty workspace shows a prompt to enrol your first machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a machine">
    Click a machine card. The detail view has tabs for **Overview, Containers, Resources, Networks, Volumes, Logs, Events,
    Security** and **Configuration** (which shows drift between what you asked for and what is running).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at your Beaver Cloud">
    Open **Beaver Cloud** in the sidebar and choose a cloud: its overview, nodes, network, capacity, services and a timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a service and a volume">
    **Services** lists managed services; each has its status, connections, credentials (masked until you reveal them), attached
    applications and storage. **Storage** and **Networks** list volumes and networks gathered from your machines and clouds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See what an action would run">
    Use the **add** actions on the Infrastructure home. Desktop shows the exact `beaver` command and does not run anything for you
    — copy it into a terminal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check approvals">
    **Approvals** lists actions waiting for a decision. In a team workspace you can approve or reject a request here.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

You can find any machine, cloud, service, volume or network from one navigator, and you know which actions to take in the
CLI or Dashboard.
