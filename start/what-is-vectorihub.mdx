**Beaver** is a deployment platform for applications that ship as Docker images. You keep your machines, your provider
and your Dockerfile; Beaver adds a consistent way to connect machines, build and deploy, put services next to your
app, secure access, and recover when something goes wrong.

## VectorIHub and Beaver

**VectorIHub** is the platform and company; **Beaver** is the name of the product you use. Every interface carries
the Beaver name: **Beaver CLI**, **Beaver Desktop**, the Dashboard, **Beaver Cloud** (a group of your machines),
**Beaver Edge** (routing and performance controls in front of an app), and managed **Services**. Alie is Beaver's
infrastructure assistant. These docs say "Beaver" for the product and name the specific piece when it matters.

## What Beaver does not do

- It is **not a hosting provider**. Your infrastructure provider bills you for machines; Beaver's plan covers Beaver.
- It does **not generate a Dockerfile or detect your framework**. A Dockerfile is required.
- It does not change your app to fit a platform: the same image runs wherever you point it.
