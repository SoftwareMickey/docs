## What you'll do

Make your workspace a **team workspace**, invite a teammate with a role, and watch the production safeguard work. Nothing you
already have moves — projects, machines, services and secrets stay where they are.

<Note>
Only the workspace **Owner** can buy a plan, and Team is a paid plan. If you are not the Owner, ask them with
`beaver billing request-upgrade --plan team`; nothing is charged by a request. See [Plans and billing](/teams/plans-and-billing).
</Note>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the workspace">
    ```bash
    beaver workspace show
    ```
    Shows the active workspace, your role, its plan and seats. Switch with `beaver workspace use <name>` if you belong to several.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Team">
    Follow [Create a team](/teams/create-a-team) — price it with `beaver billing quote`, then check out. Your workspace converts
    in place with you as Owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a teammate">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Dashboard">Open **Team → Members** and invite by email with a role.</Tab>
      <Tab title="Desktop"><Note>Inviting is not available in Desktop — use the Dashboard or the CLI.</Note></Tab>
      <Tab title="CLI">
        ```bash
        beaver workspace invite ada@example.com --role developer
        ```
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
    They receive an email with a link. See [Invite members](/teams/invite-members).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the safeguard">
    A **Developer** can deploy freely everywhere except **protected environments** (production), where they must ask an Owner or
    Admin. The request waits up to 24 hours; **you cannot approve your own request**. See
    [Production approvals](/teams/production-approvals).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide a request">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Dashboard">**Team → Approvals** lists requests; approve or reject with an optional note.</Tab>
      <Tab title="Desktop">**Approvals** lists requests; approve or reject there.</Tab>
      <Tab title="CLI">
        ```bash
        beaver workspace approvals
        beaver workspace approvals approve <id> --note "ship it"
        ```
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

Your team has an Owner, a Developer and a working approval path for production.
