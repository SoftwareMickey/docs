**Data**, **Networking & security**, **Operate**, **Teams** or **Alie** — and every how-to links to its concept. Read these first if you are new:

## Deploy

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Integrations" icon="rocket" href="/deploy/integrations">Connect GitHub so Beaver can see your repositories, deploy from them, and keep pulling new commits.</Card>
  <Card title="Configuration" icon="rocket" href="/deploy/configuration">Your deployment settings live in one local file, and Beaver keeps a saved copy per container — the two can drift, and Beaver shows you exactly how.</Card>
  <Card title="Deployments" icon="rocket" href="/deploy/deployment-lifecycle">A deployment is one service running in one environment — deploying is shipping a new build to it, with your choice of how the switch happens.</Card>
  <Card title="Projects" icon="rocket" href="/deploy/projects">A project groups one application's environments, deployments, and services — the unit almost everything else nests inside.</Card>
  <Card title="Building applications" icon="rocket" href="/deploy/builds-and-images">Beaver builds and deploys from a Dockerfile you provide — every language and framework works the same way.</Card>
  <Card title="Containers and replicas" icon="rocket" href="/deploy/containers-and-replicas">A deployment is the definition; a container is one running copy of it. Replica count is how many copies exist.</Card>
  <Card title="Scaling and resources" icon="rocket" href="/deploy/scaling-and-resources">Two separate levers control capacity: how many copies run, and how much each one gets. Fixed mode holds a count; automatic mode holds a range.</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Teams and governance

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Accounts, workspaces and teams" icon="users" href="/teams/accounts-workspaces-teams">Your account is one login; a workspace owns your projects, machines, secrets and plan; a team workspace lets several people work in it, each with a role.</Card>
  <Card title="Production safety and approvals" icon="users" href="/teams/production-approvals">A team workspace protects production by default: Developers ask before they deploy there, and an Owner or Admin decides.</Card>
  <Card title="Access, audit and removal" icon="users" href="/teams/audit-history">How removing someone takes effect, what the activity log records, and what admins can and cannot see.</Card>
  <Card title="Settings and who wins" icon="users" href="/teams/settings-and-precedence">The same setting can be set in your repository, on a project, in a workspace or for one deploy — Beaver applies a fixed order, tells you where each value came from, and refuses changes that would be silently ignored.</Card>
  <Card title="Signing in vs. connecting repositories" icon="users" href="/teams/signing-in-and-repository-access">Signing in with Google or GitHub proves who you are. Connecting repositories is a separate, explicit choice about what Beaver may read.</Card>
