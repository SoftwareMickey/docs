Welcome to VectorIHub! This guide walks you through deploying your first application — built from your own Dockerfile — to the infrastructure of your choice.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have the following:

* A VectorIHub account (Sign up at [vectorihub.com](https://vectorihub.com))
* A GitHub repository containing a `Dockerfile`
* An infrastructure target — a VPS you manage, or a supported cloud provider (AWS, Azure, DigitalOcean)

<Warning>
  A `Dockerfile` in your repository is required. See [Dockerfile Requirements](/deploying/dockerfile-requirements) for what VectorIHub expects it to define.
</Warning>

---

## 4 Steps to Launch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your Repository">
    Log in to the [VectorIHub Dashboard](https://vectorihub.com) and click **Your Project**. Select **deploy repo**.

    <Info>
      VectorIHub builds your Docker image directly from your repository's `Dockerfile`.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure your Deployment">
    Set your port, environment variables, and health check path.

    <CodeGroup>
    ```yaml .env (Example)
    PORT=5173
    DATABASE_URL=https://example.database.com
    ```
    </CodeGroup>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Your Infrastructure">
    Select where this deployment should run — a VPS you manage, or a supported cloud provider.

    <Info>
      See [Infrastructure Providers](/infrastructure) for what's supported today.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deploy & Monitor">
    Click **"Deploy"**. VectorIHub builds your image from your Dockerfile and deploys it to your chosen infrastructure.

    <Check>
      Your app will be live once the build and deployment complete!
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>
