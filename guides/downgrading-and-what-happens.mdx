Leaving Team is safe and reversible. The worst outcome of any billing change is "limited until you pay again". It is never
"lost data" or "lost people".

## Cancel, or schedule a downgrade

Only the workspace **Owner** can change the plan.

```bash
beaver billing cancel        # stop renewal; keep Team until the period you paid for ends
beaver billing downgrade     # preview what changes, then schedule a move to Free
```

- **Cancel** keeps Team until the end of the current period. Seat packs stop renewing too. Nothing is refunded.
- **Downgrade** first shows you exactly what would change: which members would be paused, which retention limits will apply, and
  what is over the new limits. After you confirm, the workspace moves to Free **at the end of the period you already paid for**.
  Only Free is available as a downgrade target.

While a cancellation or downgrade is pending, Beaver Desktop and the dashboard show when access ends.

## What happens when Team ends

| | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **People** | Members beyond the new plan's seats are **suspended**, not removed. The Owner and the earliest-joined members stay active, up to the limit. Suspended people cannot act (`membership suspended`), do not use a seat, and keep their role. |
| **Invitations** | Pending invitations are cancelled and their seats released. |
| **Approvals** | Pending approval requests expire. |
| **Your apps and data** | Untouched. Everything keeps running. |
| **Anything over a limit** | Kept, not deleted or stopped. You can only **add** more once you are back under the limit. |
| **History** | Logs, traces and the activity log follow the shorter retention of the new plan. Older entries are cleared over time. |
| **The workspace itself** | Stays a team workspace, with its history, even with a single member. It does not silently turn back into a personal one, and it does not need to. |

<Note>
  A failed payment does not do any of this. While a payment is **past due** you keep full access, with notices, until the
  gateway gives up on the subscription.
</Note>

## Come back

**Subscribing again restores everyone.** Suspended members are reactivated in the order they were paused, and each
change is recorded in the activity log. Buying more seats does the same up to the new limit.

```bash
beaver billing checkout --plan team --gateway paystack
```

## See what a change will do

```bash
beaver billing subscription
beaver workspace show
```

show the plan, seats used and included, and any limit you are over.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | Meaning and fix |
| --- | --- |
| `membership suspended` | A plan change left fewer seats than people. The Owner can add seats or resubscribe. |
| `only a downgrade to Free is available` | Downgrade to Free, then buy the plan you want. |
| `A downgrade to Free is already scheduled for <date>` | Nothing more to do. |
