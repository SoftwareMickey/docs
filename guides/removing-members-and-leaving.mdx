People change teams. Beaver makes removal immediate and safe: access ends at once, and nothing that person
created is deleted.

## Remove a member

Owners and Admins can remove members, but never someone who ranks above them.

```bash
beaver workspace remove ada@example.com
```

This takes effect **immediately**:

- Their open log streams and shells are closed.
- Their next request in this workspace is refused.
- Any API key they made for this workspace stops working.
- Their **other** workspaces and their account are untouched.

What stays: their projects, deployments and settings belong to the workspace, and a deployment already in progress
is not interrupted. The [activity log](/concepts/team-access-and-audit) keeps every action they took, with their name and the role they
held at the time. Removing them frees their seat, and you can invite them again later.

## Leave a workspace

```bash
beaver workspace leave
```

Anyone can leave, with the same effect as being removed. The exception is the last Owner: **you cannot leave a workspace
you are the only Owner of.** Transfer ownership first, or delete the workspace.

## Hand over ownership

Only the workspace's primary Owner can offer it, and only to an active **Admin or Owner**. It is an **offer**, not a
hand-over: the person must accept within **72 hours**, so nobody becomes the owner, and responsible for billing, by surprise.

```bash
beaver workspace transfer grace@example.com --confirm-name acme-team
beaver workspace transfers            # offers you have made or received
beaver workspace transfers --cancel   # withdraw your pending offer
```

The person you offered it to accepts or declines:

```bash
beaver workspace accept-transfer
beaver workspace decline-transfer
```

Nothing moves when they accept. Every project, container and setting stays exactly where it is; only who owns the
workspace changes. You stay on as an Admin, unless you pass `--leave`.

**Billing.** A Team subscription is paid with the previous owner's payment method, so the command tells you that and
asks you to confirm. After the transfer the subscription and any seat packs stop renewing; the workspace keeps its plan
until the period already paid for ends, and the new Owner subscribes with their own payment method. A failing payment
blocks the transfer until it is resolved.

See [`beaver workspace`](/cli/workspace#transfer) for the flags and failure codes.

## Before deleting your account

A person who owns a workspace that other people belong to cannot delete their account until they transfer
or remove that workspace.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | Meaning and fix |
| --- | --- |
| `a workspace must keep at least one active owner` | Make another person an Owner first. |
| `the workspace's primary owner cannot be demoted or removed; transfer ownership first` | Use `beaver workspace transfer`. |
| `you are the only owner: transfer ownership or delete the workspace instead of leaving` | Same. |
| `you cannot change or remove a member with a higher role than yours, or grant a higher role than yours` | Ask an Owner. |
