## Welcome to VectorIHub

VectorIHub is a deployment platform, not another cloud hosting provider. You define your application once — with a Dockerfile — and VectorIHub deploys it consistently, whether the target is a VPS, AWS, Azure, DigitalOcean, or another provider.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Bring Your Own Dockerfile" icon="docker" color="#16A34A">
    Your Dockerfile is the single source of truth for how your app is built and run — no guessing at your build process.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deploy Anywhere" icon="globe" color="#07C983">
    The same application deploys consistently to whichever infrastructure provider you choose.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The VectorIHub Pipeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Link your GitHub repository (Public or Private) to the VectorIHub dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build">
    VectorIHub builds a Docker image from your repository's Dockerfile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure">
    Set your port, environment variables, and health checks once in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy">
    Choose your target infrastructure — VectorIHub deploys your image there and keeps the experience consistent across providers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Core Philosophy

> **Build once. Deploy anywhere.**
>
> Your application shouldn't need to be redesigned because you changed infrastructure providers. VectorIHub keeps your deployment experience the same regardless of where it runs.

## Key Features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Dockerfile-Based Builds" icon="docker">
    Your Dockerfile already describes your dependencies, build process, runtime, and startup command — VectorIHub builds from it directly, so builds are predictable and language-agnostic.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Infrastructure Portability" icon="shuffle">
    Deploy the same application to a VPS, AWS, Azure, or DigitalOcean without redesigning your deployment workflow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Multi-Environment CI/CD" icon="layer-group">
    Manage Development, Staging, and Production environments seamlessly. Test changes safely before promoting to production.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Zero-Downtime Deploys" icon="shield-check">
    Blue-green deployments by default — your previous version stays live until the new one passes health checks.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
