## Features

Once your Dockerfile is in place, VectorIHub handles the rest — building your image, deploying it consistently, and keeping it healthy in production.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Dockerfile-Based Builds" icon="docker">
    Your Dockerfile is the single source of truth — VectorIHub builds directly from it. No guessing at your build process, no hidden platform-specific logic.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Deploy to Any Infrastructure" icon="globe">
    The same application deploys consistently to a VPS, AWS, Azure, DigitalOcean, or another supported provider.
  </Card>

  <Card title="GitHub Integration" icon="github">
    Connect your repository and VectorIHub builds and deploys automatically on every push.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Environment Variables" icon="key">
    Manage secrets and configuration from the dashboard, injected into your container at runtime — never baked into your image.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Zero-Downtime Deploys" icon="shield">
    Blue-green deployments by default, with health checks gating every rollout.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Snapshots & Rollbacks" icon="history">
    Every deployment is versioned. Test safely and roll back instantly with one click.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a Dockerfile">
    Define your build process, runtime, and startup command in a `Dockerfile`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect & Configure">
    Connect your repository and set your port, environment variables, and health checks in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Infrastructure & Deploy">
    Pick where your app runs — VectorIHub builds your image and deploys it there.
  </Step>
</Steps>
