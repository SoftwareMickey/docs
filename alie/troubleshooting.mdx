## Alie has no model or does not answer

1. `beaver alie status` — connected providers and their models. None listed means no usable connection.
2. `beaver ai providers`, then `beaver ai provider test <provider-id>` for a cloud-stored key.
3. `beaver ai models --provider <name>` — is the model you chose available?

**Fix:** connect a provider ([Connect an AI provider](/alie/connect-an-ai-provider)). Only providers shown as usable in `beaver ai connect` will work; others are listed but not enabled.

## It stopped part-way, or refuses to continue

A **spend cap** may have been reached: `beaver alie spend-cap` shows the limits and today's spend. Raise it or wait ([Spending controls](/alie/spending-controls)).

## An action it proposed did not run

It is probably **waiting for approval**. `beaver alie approvals` lists everything pending; approve or reject it. In a team workspace, a protected environment may additionally need an
Owner or Admin ([Production approvals](/teams/production-approvals)). `beaver alie audit` shows what ran and what did not.

## It did something I did not expect

Check the autopilot level (`beaver alie autopilot`) and per-tool overrides (`beaver alie approval-policies`), then the audit trail. Lower the level and require approval:
[Safe production use](/alie/safe-production-use).

## An answer was wrong

`beaver alie report` reports that a harness task's result was wrong.

## Still stuck

Collect the output of `beaver alie status` and `beaver doctor`, then contact support at support@vectorihub.com.
