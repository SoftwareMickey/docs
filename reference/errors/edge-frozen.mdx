Beaver has **parked Beaver Edge** — the product that put a Beaver edge node in front of an app Beaver does not deploy, for routing, firewall rules, request logs and alerts. Creating or changing a connected app answers
with the code `EDGE_FROZEN` (HTTP 403) and sends you here, and `beaver edge …` does nothing except show this page.

This is a pause, not a verdict: we narrowed Beaver to a surface one team can run well before widening it again.

## What is not affected

- **Your connected apps keep working.** DNS, routing and proxying are untouched, and your apps remain readable.
- **The deployment firewall is unchanged.** `beaver firewall` and the firewall tab on a deployment protect apps Beaver deploys, and keep working.
- **Beaver Cloud ingress is unchanged.** Apps deployed on a Beaver Cloud keep their stable address.

## What to do instead

Deploy the app on Beaver (`beaver deploy`) to get routing, the deployment firewall, logs and health checks, or keep your own reverse proxy in front of it.
