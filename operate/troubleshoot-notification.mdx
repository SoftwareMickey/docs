## What you see

An alert you expected did not reach you.

## Check first

1. `beaver notifications --unread` — it may be in your inbox even if no message reached you.
2. `beaver workspace digest` — is a digest holding non-urgent items?
3. `beaver workspace on-call` — who an alert would actually reach right now.

## Causes and fixes

### It is waiting in a digest

Non-urgent notifications are bundled; **critical and security notifications are never held for a digest**.

### The wrong person is on call

`beaver workspace on-call` shows the same resolution the system uses to page people.

### Severity is lower than you assumed

Filter by severity: `beaver notifications --severity=warning`.

## Still stuck

Run `beaver doctor` on the affected machine and collect its output, then contact support at support@vectorihub.com.
