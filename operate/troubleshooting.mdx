Find the thing you are looking at, not the thing you think is wrong. Every page starts with the fastest checks and ends with where to send what you collected.

<Tip>
**Not sure where to start?** Run `beaver doctor` on the affected machine — it checks the system, network, Docker, Git, sign-in, workspace, registration and agent, and changes nothing —
and `beaver inspect` in the project.
</Tip>
