An **incident** is an operational problem Beaver has detected and is tracking. It has a **status** — `open`, `monitoring` or `resolved` — and a timeline of what happened.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">Open **Incidents** to see them, open one for its timeline, and acknowledge it.</Tab>
  <Tab title="Desktop"><Note>Not a separate section in Desktop. Machine alerts and events appear on a machine's **Events** and **Security** tabs.</Note></Tab>
  <Tab title="CLI">
    ```bash
    beaver incidents --status=open
    beaver incidents show <id>               # full timeline
    beaver incidents acknowledge <id>        # the same action as the Dashboard
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Acknowledging** records that you have seen it; it does not fix anything. To act, use the [recovery](/deploy/rollback-and-recovery) tools, and `beaver recover` for a read-only diagnosis first.
