## Deploy Anywhere

VectorIHub builds your application from your Dockerfile once, then deploys it to the infrastructure provider you choose. Your application definition doesn't change when your target infrastructure does.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="VPS" icon="server" href="/providers/vps">

  </Card>
  <Card title="AWS" icon="aws" href="/providers/aws">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Azure" icon="microsoft" href="/providers/azure">

  <Card title="DigitalOcean" icon="digital-ocean" href="/providers/digitalocean">

## The Same Workflow, Every Provider

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a Dockerfile">
    Define your build process, runtime, and startup command in a `Dockerfile`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Your Deployment">
    Set your port, environment variables, and health checks once in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Infrastructure">
    Pick the provider this deployment should run on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy">
    VectorIHub builds your image and deploys it to your chosen provider.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You retain ownership and control of your own infrastructure and cloud resources — VectorIHub coordinates deployment onto them, rather than hosting your app on VectorIHub-owned infrastructure.
</Note>
