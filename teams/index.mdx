A **workspace** owns your projects, machines, secrets and plan. A **team workspace** lets several people work in it, each
with a role, with production protected by default.

<Tip>
**Start here:** [Workspaces and teams](/teams/accounts-workspaces-teams) for the model, then
[create a team](/teams/create-a-team).
</Tip>
