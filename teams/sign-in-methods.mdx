A **sign-in method** is a way to prove who you are to your Beaver account: your email, Google, or GitHub. You can have more than one on the same account. Signing in is **separate from
repository access** — see [Signing in vs. connecting repositories](/teams/signing-in-and-repository-access) for that distinction and for every message each flow can show.

## See your methods

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">**Settings → Security** lists the ways you can sign in.</Tab>
  <Tab title="Desktop">Desktop signs you in through your browser (see [Install Beaver Desktop](/install/desktop)); manage methods in the Dashboard.</Tab>
  <Tab title="CLI">
    ```bash
    beaver whoami --methods     # also shows which sign-in methods this account has (one network call)
    beaver github               # your sign-in methods and each connected GitHub account's health
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Add or remove one

- **Add Google or GitHub** to an existing account from **Settings → Security**. If you choose *Continue with Google* on the sign-in page and the Google email matches your account, you are offered an emailed link
  to your Beaver address that connects it once you confirm.
- **Remove** one from the same page. Beaver will not let you remove your **only** way to sign in.
- Beaver recognises your Google account by its permanent id, not its address, so changing your Google email does not lock you out.

## Sign in from the CLI or Desktop

The CLI and Desktop never ask for a password or token. `beaver login` (and Desktop's **Sign in**) opens a browser approval page — **Approve this device** — where you sign in with any of your methods and approve the connection.
On a server with no browser, `beaver login --no-browser` prints the link to open on any device. If 2-step verification is on, the approval page asks for it.

## Protect the account

Turn on [2-step verification](/teams/two-step-verification); an Owner can require it for a workspace.
